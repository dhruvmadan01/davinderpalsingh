What’s actually happening

A life has seven aspects. Most people chase exactly one of them — money, and the things money buys — and quietly lose the other six.

Work happens in force, not flow. Teeth gritted, from the alarm in the morning to the end of the day.

Sleep is broken. The body runs on tablets and still doesn’t feel right.

Children grow up and stop getting along with the parents who raised them. It is the same story in house after house.

These are not four separate problems. The same thing is missing from all of them — structure.

The ten levels

Ground up. Each level is the foundation of the one above it, and the order cannot be changed.

FoundationLevels 1–3

1

Deep Sleep

11 days

Everything starts here. When sleep isn’t whole, the body doesn’t repair and the mind won’t hold still. Eleven days to put sleep back where it belongs.

2

MBA with Nature Mind Body Alignment with Nature

21 days

Twenty-one days of daily practice that puts mind and body back in step with nature’s own rhythm — how you wake, how you eat, how you breathe, how you spend the day.

3

Holistic Healing

66 days

Understanding the body — why it breaks down, and what helps it recover. Practical, at home, through daily living rather than a cabinet of remedies. What you learn here doesn’t stop with you; it carries to your whole family and the generations after them.

SelfLevels 4–5

4

Flow State

Most people work from a force state — every task pushed through under pressure, by compulsion. Flow is the opposite. Work, relationships, a function at home, anything: you move through it without grinding.

5

Intimacy Mastery

For married and adult participants

Understanding the energy of married life. People reach for tablets and one medicine after another to meet desire, and neither the depth nor the satisfaction ever arrives. This level works on the understanding instead — ease, restraint, and real connection.

FamilyLevels 6–7

6

Conscious Conceiving

For couples who want to carry forward what they have built. Rather than leaving a child to chance, preparing deliberately for one whose energy matches both parents and whose direction aligns with theirs.

7

Conscious Parenting

What a parent’s own nature needs to be, what to keep a child occupied with from the very start, and how their mindset takes shape. Whether the relationship survives their growing up is decided right here.

LifeLevel 8

8

The Seven Aspects of Life

Money is one of the seven. What the other six are, why they slip away, and how to carry all seven at once. This is where the first seven levels come together.

ExpansionLevels 9–10

9

Train the Trainer

Anyone who gets this far is no longer only a student — they become a coach. How to teach it, and how to fold your own experience back into the system.

10

Ecosystem

A full structure for coaches — organic reach on social media, a platform to teach from, and a steady income from the work. Not on your own; with the whole system behind you.

How it runs

Length
The full journey runs two to three years. There is no rushing it — every level needs time to settle before the next one holds.
Method
Part of it is recorded, to work through at your own pace. Live classes run alongside throughout.
Order
Levels are taken in sequence. Deep Sleep comes first, because very little after it holds without it.
Language
Sessions are currently held in Hindi, with English cohorts opening as the programme expands.
