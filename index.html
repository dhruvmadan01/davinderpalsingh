The wound

I was a computer engineering student when the way my friends behaved left a wound in my heart. I saw how self-interested people could be with one another, and it brought tears to my eyes.

The question

Soon after, at a gurdwara, someone played me a shabad of Guru Gobind Singh Ji. As I listened, I got goosebumps. It was my last semester, yet only one thing mattered now.

What is life? What is dharma? Was I born only to grow up, marry, have children, grow old and die? Or was I born human to do something more?

Two years

I finished my degree, left home and went to live at a gurdwara. For two years I did santhya of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji and lived among sadhus. Naam, Gurbani, its meaning, katha and seva: there was nothing else in my life.

The promise

I was my parents’ only son, and I had given my word that I would come back. So I came back. After a few months at home I felt like a burden on them, and I knew I had to do something with my life.

The fall, again and again

Twenty-six businesses. Nineteen years. Seven betrayals.

I built and sold six or seven houses. I started a dairy farm on one acre, trained in artificial insemination and treated my animals with my own hands. When the farm ran at a loss I opened a retail counter in the city, then a collection centre. Then sweets, bakery, confectionery, fast food, imported fruit, juices, paneer.

I failed again and again. Seven times, partners betrayed me badly. Differences came between me and my parents. Buildings went up and machinery came in, but in nineteen years money never stayed in my account.

What was being built

Outside, it looked like being stuck. Inside, so much was being built. I had lived in the world of the spirit and in the world of business. I had learned what betrayal is, and who stands beside you when life turns hard. Slowly I began to feel that the universe itself was teaching me, personally.

I was a student of the University of Universe.

The first class

Then two relatives came to my home. Their health was poor and their minds were in pain, so I began to teach them. They told others, and eleven people came. Three of them were in a very hard place. I watched them become happier, find interest in their work again and begin to treat their families with love. They gave blessings to me and to my children.

The flow

While teaching I would begin to sweat, even in cold weather with the fan running. Energy that had been stuck in me was finally moving. All that life had given me, I had never shared. When I began to share it, the people in front of me changed, and so did I. My work began to move. For the last eleven months, I have not felt unhappy for a single day.

In twenty-six businesses, not once did anyone say to me: you have fallen, now get up.

I would like to say that to you.

Does this sound familiar?

A life has seven aspects. You may be chasing one of them — money, and the things money buys — while the other six quietly slip.

Your work happens in force, not flow. Teeth gritted, from the alarm in the morning to the end of the day.

Your sleep is broken. You run on tablets and still don’t feel right.

Your children are growing up, and the distance between you is growing too. It is the same story in house after house.

These are not four separate problems. The same thing is missing from all of them — structure.

The ten levels

Ground up, because that is the order in which a person changes: sleep, then the body, then the mind, then relationships, then children, and only then the ability to guide someone else. Open any level to see what it involves.

FoundationLevels 1–3

98 days in all. First, a rested and steady body.

1

Deep Sleep

11 days

Everything starts here. When sleep isn’t whole, the body doesn’t repair and the mind won’t hold still. Eleven days of practice to start putting sleep back where it belongs.

What happens at this level

Why here. Comes first because the body repairs during sleep, and everything after this asks for attention and steadiness that a tired person doesn’t have.

Objective
Build a steady sleep routine and understand what helps your own sleep go deeper.
Activities
Eleven days of guided daily practice through recorded lessons and live classes, built into your own evenings and mornings.
Duration
11 days of practice.
Progress
You keep a short daily note of when you slept, how often you woke and how rested you felt, then compare day 11 with day 1. There is no pass mark.
Evidence & limitations
We have not published outcome data for this level yet. Eleven days is the length of the practice, not a promise of the result. Sleep trouble has many causes and some will not change in this time. If you snore loudly, gasp at night, or have had trouble sleeping for months, please see a doctor. Keep taking prescribed medicines unless your doctor changes them.
2

What happens at this level

Why here. Once sleep is steadier, there is energy to change the daily habits that sit around it.

Objective
Bring how you wake, eat, breathe and spend the day into a rhythm that suits your body.
Activities
Twenty-one days of daily practice covering waking, eating, breathing and how the day is spent.
Duration
21 days of practice.
Progress

Evidence & limitations
We have not published outcome data for this level yet. This is a lifestyle practice. If you have a health condition, are pregnant, or take regular medicine, check with your doctor before changing how you eat or breathe in practice.

Questions people ask

Do I have to commit to two or three years?

No. Two to three years is how long the whole journey takes. You begin with Deep Sleep, which is eleven days, and decide about the next level after that.

What does it cost?

The Manifestation Calculator is free. Fees for the levels are not listed here yet, so please ask on WhatsApp and you will have the answer before you decide anything.

Will eleven days fix my sleep?

It may help, but nobody can promise that. Eleven days is the length of the practice. Sleep trouble has many causes, and if yours is long-standing or worrying, please see a doctor.

Should I stop my medicines?

No. Please do not stop or change any medicine because of something you read or learn here. Talk to your doctor first.

Is this medical treatment or therapy?

No. This coaching is for lifestyle, understanding and personal development. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, and it does not replace your doctor or a mental health professional.

How are the sessions delivered?

Part of it is recorded, to work through at your own pace, with live classes alongside throughout.

Are the Intimacy Mastery and Conscious Conceiving levels for everyone?

Intimacy Mastery is for married and adult participants. Conscious Conceiving is for couples who want to prepare for a child. If a level doesn’t apply to your life, ask on WhatsApp how it works for you.

Is the manifestation score scientific?

No. It is a structured way to reflect on your own answers. It hasn’t been scientifically validated, and it doesn’t predict whether you will reach your goal.
