One outcome at a time. The whole check looks at your relationship with this one.

How the score works

You answer two questions in each of nine dimensions, from 0 to 10. The two answers are averaged, the average out of 10 is multiplied by that dimension’s weight, and the nine results are added to make a score out of 100.

Action carries 25 of the 100 points and Focus 20. Exposure and Conception carry 10 each, Feeling, Intention and Belief 8 each, Consistency 6 and Adoptability 5.

The weights show what this coaching framework emphasises. They have not been statistically validated.

This is a reflection tool, not a prediction. The score is not the chance of reaching your goal. It reflects what you tell it about your current habits and mindset, and it cannot know your circumstances, your constraints or your luck.
